[div][attr="class","submission"]World Contribution Submission - Bêlu

[u]Bêlu Name[/u] Please note that your House will be of noble rank which means it will be referred to as Bêl'-Name-. Please try to use a name that does not begin with the same letter / sounds too alike to an already existing house. This helps make the House name memorable to our members.

[u]Bêlu Sigil[/u] What sigil or crest would this House have upon its shield? Please note that this is subject to availability - check our Houses Registry (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/595/) to avoid suggesting duplicates.

[u]Bêlu Colours[/u] What colours would the House shield have? Please note that this is subject to availability - check our Houses Registry (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/595/) to avoid suggesting duplicates.

[u]Bêlu Motto[/u] This needs to be a few words that detail the general feeling of the House. For example, the Gesin of Ubuntu prides itself on being a compassionate tribe but also grand hunters of wildlife. Their motto is “Take Heart". Something that can be read in more than one fashion and which is distinctive to the group. Try to keep this phrase as just a few words (and no more than five-ish at max).

[u]Province of Rule[/u] Bêlus preside over one provincial parcel of land. This is a land they own but they are required, annually, to pledge by oath their peerage and resources to the crown. Please select one of the provinces listed on the World Contribution page (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/586) as available in Assyria. Click the name of the province to read up on its statistics / details. Make sure you have chosen a land that works well with your Bêlu idea.

[u]Reason for Choice[/u] Please state your reasons for choosing this particular province(s). What plotting opportunities have you considered when choosing this land? How does the land connect with the characters as part of your House? E.g., if you have chosen a land-locked province, it is unlikely that the eldest son of the Angal is a sailor (for example). Your characters and house should weave into the fabric of the provincial land lore. Make your choice carefully and tell us what kind of plotting drama could occur between this house and its land of rule...

[u]Bêlu Blurb[/u] This needs to be roughly 250 words regarding the House, its recent history or origin (if either are particularly note-worthy) and its general feel, ethos, trade, purpose and intent. Think of this blurb as a summary of everything else in this section of your House app. If you are uncertain what to write in this, please check our Houses Registry (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/595) to see other examples.

[u]Reason for Becoming Noble[/u] Why was this family granted nobility and made a Bêlu? When did it happen? Are they a long-standing noble house or recently elevated to the status? Please include a date (in BC) when this House was first established as noble and who gave them that nobility (and for what reason). Ideas might include a member of the family doing one thing particularly note-worthy for the royal house Kushiq, or perhaps longevity of loyal service finally paid off, or the family wanting to become noble and making it happen through bribery / blackmail... The only limit is your creativity.

[u]Origin / Appearance[/u] Where were your House's founders originally from and what is the general appearance of your family's aesthetics (for most submissions this will simply be 'Middle Eastern' ). Please check out our Face Claims list (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/529) for help on which areas would have produced which ethnical appearances during this era.

[u]Dominant Features[/u] Consider the face claims you have chosen for your family's characters. What are the dominant traits? These might be facial features, height or build, or even personality traits if they are particularly strong. This helps others to add to this house and keep to their characteristics if they want to create cousins, siblings or claim one of your house's wanted ads in the future.

[u]Bêlu Ethos[/u] Please give a few morals or values that the House believes in. These should be short and sweet sentences that sum up their House's main beliefs. E.g., 'Vows are unbreakable.' 'Money is the greatest power.' etc...

[u]Reputation[/u] Please choose four singular words (not sentences) that others might use to describe the members of this House. These might not be descriptors the House themselves would use: it is about how others see them. Are they 'arrogant', 'well-connected', 'wealthy', 'stoic' etc.?

[u]Military Unit[/u] Please copy and paste the name of the military unit of your preferred province(s) here. You can find this in the province's roleplay board or the appropriate Land page in the Encyclopaedia.

[u]Trade Exports[/u] Please copy and paste the main and sub-exports of your preferred province(s) here. You can find this in the province's roleplay board or the appropriate Land page in the Encyclopaedia.

[u]Politics[/u] Please write a small paragraph about how this House is politically involved in the world. Are they loyal to their monarch? Do they walk the rebellious line? Do they refuse to partake in wider political games and keep to their own, smaller hub of power? Do they have conflict with other Houses/vassals. Do they have an entirely different focus like maritime trade or the military?

[u]Military[/u] To what extent does this House utilise the military service in its province(s)? Do they have a specialty in their weaponry or fighting style? Use the description of the province(s) in the appropriate Land page of the Encyclopaedia, or on their roleplay boards, to help you with this. An area that specialises in forestry and timber exports, for example, are not going to be skilled chariot fighters - they'd have limited open land on which to practice.

[u]Economics[/u] What are the primary trades of your province(s)? Why those? Is it to do with the land, the climate, the men? What does your House think of these trades? Are they looking to expand, happy with their lot, wanting more money or happy to be specialists? Again, use the appropriate Land page, or the province's roleplay board, to help you with these details. You should keep true to the information that is already listed but are welcome (and encouraged) to expand upon it.

[u]Preferred Plots[/u] Please write a little in here about what kind of global plots you would like this House to be a part of. What prompted the creation of this House? How can they fit into the global plot we currently have on-going? This information will help the staff team to create Events and threads in which the members of this House can participate, so you can explore the plots you want to.

[u]Character List[/u] Please list all of the characters within this House, played or not. All House submissions must have 4+ living characters within their family tree; this is the creation of an entire family, not a single individual, nor the sole survivor of a bloodline. The tree can consist of the immediate family (those who hold the name of the House) but also the cousins and additional branches that would hold only the name of the province in which they were born. So long as there are four (or more), living and playable characters tied to this family by blood. Please list them in a way that makes sense to their order of birth. E.g., something like this:

Grandad Character
Grandma Character
= Dad Character
m. Mom Character
=== First Born Character
=== Second Born Character
= Uncle Character
m. Aunt Character
=== First Born Cousin Character

You can include deceased characters in this list, in order to help explain how others are related but across the whole list there should be four or more characters still living.

Once these characters are listed, please copy, paste and fill in the following code for each and every living family member listed, beginning with the current leader (Angal) of the family. If the house is accepted, these characters will be immediately turned into Wanted Ads for the House.

[u]Age[/u] Self-explanatory.
[u]Occupation[/u] Self-explanatory. Most will be 'Courtiers'.
[u]Connection to the Angal[/u] This is the connection to the Angal himself, rather than the house - son, daughter, nephew etc. If this character *is* the Angal, just delete this line.
[u]Nationality[/u] Self-explanatory. Most will be 'Syrian'.
[u]Province of Birth[/u] Self-explanatory. This does not have to be the province ruled by the House if they were born elsewhere.
[u]Religion[/u] Self-explanatory. Most Syrians will be 'Polyassyric'.
[u]Face Claim[/u] Please check our Face Claim list (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/529) to ensure that this face is not already chosen for another played, canon or wanted character. If you have any confusion over this, please message a staff member regarding your choices.
[u]Character Blurb[/u] This blurb must read as a wanted ad, for that is what it will become upon the acceptance of the House. It must be over 300 words in length and cover the character's basic history, familial connections, personality, hobbies etc. and any plot potential they hold. Remember: use the information you have written above for the House and its province(s) to aid in the creation of the character. Who are the people who have the economic ambitions you might have written of? Does the son of the baron wish to join the military because the military in its province(s) is known to be particular poor and he wants to turn it around? Remember to ensure that these characters are written in a manner that befits the environment that they grew up in. The characters should feel as synonymous with the House as the House does with the land.

A Note Regarding Face Claims: Please be aware that Aeipathy take great pride in choosing face claims within family groups that bear a strong resemblance to one another. If your face claims do not, in the eyes of the staff team, appear believable in terms of their relationships, age or fictional genetics, please be aware that you may be asked to change them.

[u]Canon Connections[/u] In order to ensure that our contributions do not get cut off from our canon world, we require all of our Houses to have two connections to canons in some way, shape or form. For example... one of your House's characters might have a rivalry with a royal son who they trained with in the militia, another might be a secret admirer of a royal lady in court or had an affair with said lady to the shame and scandal of all. The connection can be with a particular character or a House as a whole. But there must be two of them and they cannot be with the same house. We recommend that you put a shout out on our Discord server (https://discord.gg/2f2MZP3G) or seek out members who actively play canon characters (check our Compendium - https://aeipathyrp.com/board/477) in order to make this 'connection' with active players or at least with open canon characters that have a close connection to active characters. This is all about building a springboard plot for your House's launch. Make sure to include:
- What the connection is.
- Who the connection is between.
- Which members you have spoken with to finalise this idea.
- How you see it playing out in the future (this doesn't have to be set in stone - give us a range of fun ideas you intend to throw at the wall and we look forward to seeing what sticks).
