The ruling family of Babylonia has been famed, for many decades, as a house of balance and harmony. In the past, a single generation has included a victorious war lord and a peace-loving diplomat. Its women are spirited and yet elegant, its men are bold and yet thoughtful. Only in recent generations has the Ashina name been associated with scandal. After the suspicious deaths of the primary line of inheritance, the crown has fallen to a second son. And from there to a foreigner; a young woman raised from a brothel to a throne, without experience nor training. Just how far can Ashina's good name be stretched under this new leadership? Standards of Practice
PoliticsAshina is the ruling family of Babylonia so is deeply involved in the politics and ruling decisions of the land. They are the ultimate authority on Chaldean law and political matters.
TradeThough trade is not their forte, the Ashina family have a vast generational wealth ready to be invested and applied all over the land. This has brought them a strong, personal trading economy. On top of this, as the reigning household, Ashina also decide on the tax policies, trading laws and international exporting / importing regulations.
MilitaryThe monarch of Babylonia is officially and by default the leader of the centralised, national military. Even through the family itself does not have a strong military history or experience, they have full access to the entire state's military power.
ReligionAs rulers of the state, the Ashina family are also the official head of the religious system and temples. They have a strong connect to the priesthood and many of the women in the Ashina family have served in the temples.

The house of Balthasar is the youngest in Babylonia but it is by no means behind in the race of wealth and prestige. Marked with the pedigree of a king's niece and funded by highly lucrative business abroad, the speed of this family's power-grab has ensured that the Balthasar name inspires awe and uncertainty in equal measure. Excessively wealthy, with a strong trade across Babylonia, Assyria, Phoenicia and beyond, the Balthasar clan are ambitious. And many in the Chaldean Court have made their preferences clear: that the Balthasar matriarch would make a a finer choice of queen than the little girl currently daring to wear the crown. Standards of Practice
PoliticsPolitically, the Balthasar family has the equal rank to any other noble household, owning and managing several areas outside the capital. But they have ambitions for more. Power and prestige is now this family's focus.
TradeAn already conquered realm for the Balthasar house, trade is their primary expertise. With the two brothers arriving in Babylonia as the sons of merchants, trade is in their blood and they have spent their lifetimes finding and exploiting every international connection, commerce opportunity and business deal. It has made them very, very wealthy.
MilitaryBalthasar have very little connection to the military besides their income. Weaponry is one of their largest business ventures, yet they have no active part in how the equipment is used once they receive payment.
ReligionA few family members of Balthasar are involved in the temple system but this is more for personal passion or courtly etiquette - it is not the primary purpose of the house itself.

In all lands there are those who do not seek fortune nor fame. Those who are driven by loyalty over greed and integrity over glory. In the land of Babylonia, Kinsalla is just such as family. Despite a long history of military accomplishments, the Kinsalla name has never been associated with coups or rebellions. They have never fought for their own victories or power. Instead, they are widely recognised for their fealty to the Babylonian crown and for their humility in the halls of power.
