Netuno is a house of longevity. Found to the north of Roma's mainland territories, their provinces are built with legacy in mind. From fine crafts and artistry to carpentry, labour trades and scholastic pursuits, the Netuno invest in the only currency that lasts forever: knowledge. Whether it be the records of academia, the practice of handicrafts or the whisper of political rumour, the Netuno recognise the power in information and the wisdom in how to utilise it. They are experts in diplomacy, silver tongued in their exploits in the capital and open-minded to the vast array of businesses operating out of their lands. One can never know the future but you can prepare yourself for it with enough experience, expertise and education. Standards of Practice

The Remusi are a dwindling household of victorious legacy: a prestigiously plumed helmet without its shine. Once the defending sword of the Romani empire, this noble house has fallen to the last two direct descendants. Too young to be taken seriously in the Parliamentum, the Remusi brothers have banded together to share the Imperia Nobilis seat. They hold themselves to the highest of standards and the sharpest of military records in order to preserve what little influence remains of the powerhouse reputation their predecessors built for them. When the royal bloodline of Roma relinquished their right to rule, the family was divided in two. As Cannae sought to find meaning in their new liberty, Veneti held to their legacy. Though accepting of their now equalist role in the Parliamentum, the Veneti saw no advantage to denying their expertise. Generations of political domination, legal mastery and rulership over the judiciary system have equipped the Veneti well. And they now utilise that talent in service of the Romani people. No higher in official status than their fellow Imperia seats in the capital, the Veneti are still turned to for deciding votes, for the imparting of their wisdom or when diplomatic envoys arrive from other lands. From behind a mask of austere integrity, the Veneti are happy to help. Standards of Practice

Skirmishes and bloodshed stain Roma's northernmost borderlands. It is here that the Viatrix household watch the great walls and guard the battlelands. The barbarians of Ostjord skulk the boundary like wolves but Viatrix are learning their ways. Employing a careful mixture of displays of martial dominance and open diplomacy, Viatrix keeps the Roma border unbreeched and the Ostjord warriors amused. But for how long can this awkwad, violent, fluctuating truce last as the northern lands grow barer and the provinces of Roma appear all the more bountiful by contrast? For their efforts in the north, Viatrix have a well-deserved reputation founded in bravery, loyalty and a tactical intelligence possessed by few military commanders. Standards of Practice
