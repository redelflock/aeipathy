The Hei of Abaddi is the youngest house of nobility within K'm't. Only recently granted the status of nobles by the late pharaoh, the family holds no special privileges or powers over any land as yet. Instead, they look to build their support and reputation in the Council and in general upper-class society by keeping their senses alert and their heads down. PoliticsThe Abaddi are new to the K'm't social spheres, so tend to keep their ears open and their heads low. For this, they have slowly built a reputation of dignity - a repute that comes with a very small amount of social and political clout.
