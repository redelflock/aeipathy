The Hei of Abaddi is the youngest house of nobility within K'm't. Only recently granted the status of nobles by Pharaoh Iahotep after a great victory over Bedoan raiders on the western border by General Sethtah, the family holds no special privileges or powers over any land as yet. Instead, they look to build their support and reputation in the Council, taking military campaigns wherever possible and supporting the Pharaoh and his Queen in order to win the favouritism necessary to advance and gain further glory for the Abaddi name, securing the future of its children. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThe Abaddi are young to the K'm't social spheres so tend to keep their ears open and their heads low. They have learned the basic etiquette of the upper classes and work hard to never step into conversations or dynamics they are still unequipped for. For this, they have slowly built a reputation of dignity - a repute that comes with a very small amount of social and political clout.
TradeAs they have yet to seriously impress the late or current pharaoh, the Abaddi family have no special privileges in provinces nor great swathes of land from which they can profit. They have a small estate and rental areas that bring them something of an income and they are working to invest their limited means wisely.
MilitaryAs far as the military goes, the Abaddi are much more respected than their social status. Their patriarch is a skill soldier who has worked his way up from nothing through talent, tactics and tenacity. This is impossible for his peers to ignore, even if his origins are less than palatable.
ReligionReligiously, the Abaddi are not great worshippers. However, in an effort not to draw the disfavour of society or the gods, they carry out the motions of their faith with care.

The great Hei of Fakhouri was once a house of Kings. For nearly four hundred years, the Pharaohs of K'm't came from the Fakhouri Hei. And on the odd generation when the men of the Hei were not born to the next Pharaoh a woman of the Fakhouri bloodline would become their Queen. The history of this Hei is woven into the very tapestry of Kemetic history. No other Hei has the same legacy, power or might as Fakhouri, even though there is now only a single living member of the clan. At the time that K'm't last went to war with Greece - some ten years ago - Fakhouri, nearly all of its male heirs to war. With the female lines moving to marry into other Hei the name of Fakhouri never lost its power but it diminished significantly within the Council of K'm't. The last hold out is Queen Dowager Isetheperu. While technically H'Naddar, the Queen Dowager openly considers herself a Fakhouri above all else and clearly awaits the birth of many a grandchild that can take the name back and reinvigorate the life and light within the Hei. Despite being a woman, the Queen Dowager operates a rare place of power in the Council of K'm't as a woman more cunning than Pharaoh and more experienced than the Queen. She rules Fakhouri with a rod of iron in a manner so complete that one would think the Hei to be at the height of its power yet still. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThough the Fakhouri house has barely any official participants anymore, the political standing of the Queen Dowager cannot be understated. She is the longest servicing politician in the land, has hundreds of relationships through beneficiaries, blackmail or bribes and holds most of the pharaoh's Council in the palm of her hand. This power is more Isetheperu's than Fakhouri's but, in the eyes of the Queen Dowager, they are one and the same.
TradeFakhouri has a long history of trade along profitable coastlines and busy arable provinces. They have accumulated vast amounts of inter-generational wealth and have a bustling trading economy that continues to make them even richer.
MilitaryThe special privileges Fakhouri enjoys in the trading markets do not make them militarily's powerful. They have private militia forces that they can call on when necessary but do not have active military personnel in the family.
ReligionAs the Queen Dowager, Isetheperu is a key figure in many large-scale religious and annual festivities. She is devout in playing her part in the drama that is the worship of the kemetic gods.

Once a powerful house in the kingdom of Egypt, the death of its Head of House some fifteen years ago - at a time when the next in line was only thirteen years of age - set the Hei of Haikaddad on the path of downward fortune. Over this period, business contracts have been renounced, trade arrangements redirected and the taxes from promised lands have been all that has kept them from financial ruin. Now that the younger brother of the late Leader is old enough to take over and has been handling business matters for the last few years, the Hei of Haikaddad is finding its feet once more. Still showing signs of wear and tear around the edges; both in its lands and manors, the Hei is beaten but far from out. With their sights firmly set inward, on establishing the strength of their trade and connections once more, the Hei of Haikaddad is one of great heritage that is not being permitted the fade out. Standards of Practice
PoliticsHaikaddad was once a greatly-respected household and they still retain some of that grandeur. However, recent events have led them to lose a lot of their social standing or at least spread the uncertainty of their friendships and business connections (and marriage outside of K'm't has not helped). This has caused a knock to their political standing. With some, their words have the opportunity to reach the highest of circles. With others, it would be a firmly shut door. The trick is to know which is which.
TradeHaikaddad has suffered natural disaster and mismanagement of affairs over the last few generations. Now, they own a much smaller number of land parcels and have special privileges in only one province. This has limited their means of making money substantially. With many costs and few incomings, the current generation of Haikaddad are facing a much worse financial situation than they are letting on...
MilitaryMilitarily, Haikaddad is still widely respected. Both its leader and young male heir are eagerly active in military discussions and they have a long-standing repute for success on the battlefield. However, as the patriarch must now focus on issues at home, there are few chances for Haikaddad to keep their victorious image going.
ReligionDespite the side-eyes from native women, the half-Bedoan ladies of Haikaddad do offer their services to the local temples in the hopes of earning divine favour. They are, however, less devout than other households.

A Hei famous for the beautiful woman of its lineage and province, the three daughters of the current generation are widely recognised to be the most beautiful and sought after women in Egypt, with many men vying for their hand. The original financial backing for such the hose came from merchants and traders, while they have progressed up the social ladder to now become skilled politicians and courtiers. Hei Isazari has been working within these circles for generations and, while they may not be the eldest of Hei houses, their experience shows in the way they handle their social obligations. Always wanting to be at the centre of things, the Isazari family home resides in Cairo. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThe Isazari are at the pinnacle of social power. Particularly on the women's side. What is favourable, fashionable and generally accepted in the higher echelons of society is dictated by the three Isazari sisters - particularly Berenike. They are known for their influence, social power and being the gates through which all social etiquette must be approved. Add to this the household's wealthy patriarch and his place on the pharaoh's Council and you have a recipe for massive political influence.
TradeIn trade, there are few men in K'm't more skilled and wealthy than Amon'ra H'Isazari. At his touch, business turns to gold and jewels flow into his family's treasury. They are one of the wealthiest houses in K'm't and enjoy the affluence and respect such income naturally generates.
MilitaryIn military, the Isazari have only a small presence but they have yet to fail in their duties on the battlefield so, whilst their glory is not large, it still shines...
ReligionPart of the social influence the Isazari women wield is tied to the temple works of the capital. They are often seen donating their time, resources and efforts to both political and religious ceremonies and aiding the queen in her public tasks. Whether this comes from true, intentional worship or merely a show of affinity with the royal family is hard to tell.

A family of military strength and a history of decorated Generals, the Moghadam Hei is known for breeding men who are very decisive and firm when it comes to political decisions and courageous and valiant in war. As one of the longest-running Hei's in Egypt, Moghadam have connections and associations with nearly every Hei in the kingdom. A strongly male-dominated family for many years, the last few generations have been less lucky. No great plague or strife has struck the family but numbers to the Moghadam name have dwindled; nothing destructive has decimated the family, simply a natural growth that has happened after few children and general life span. The Moghadam Hei has never lost the glory and reputation attached to its history; they have always been considered with great respect in the Council of K'm't. But current times call for a change and the current Head of the Moghadam House has made waves as a powerful General abroad. Now is the time to ensure that the future of this Hei shines as brightly as its past. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThough their house is small and now comprises of only one man active in public situations, that man has great sway. Osorsen H'Moghadam is a widely sought bachelor, war hero and leader of his household. His very presence has a massive influence on the Council of Ministers and social circles alike. The long-standing, victorious reputation of the glorious house of Moghadam is being put to good use in his hands.
TradeMoghadam are not a hei focused on trade. However, as they have been inexistence for many decades, they have slowly built up a household fund that is invested carefully. Their income is more than substantial enough to keep them in a luxurious lifestyle without them having to go into the cutthroat market of the merchantile trade.
MilitaryThe military is the Moghdam hei's forte. They have traditionally been appointed to high ranks for the last five generations - including the rank of General multiple times. They are the only house in K'm't who can claim this feat and the current patriarch of the house is the most successful general in the land. Their power and authority in military circles cannot be overstated.
ReligionAs an old family with long traditions, Moghadam are very devout and take every religious or public festival very seriously. If they are uninterested in a festival, they stay out of it entirely. If they are a part of the proceedings, they carry out their duties to perfection and often over-deliver.

A House of great power but also great limitations, the Hei of Naddar is the current Royal Hei of K'm't. Having been in possession of the Pharaohdom for several generations. Prior to being a noble Hei at all, they were charged with the record keeping of government, providing the scribe services to the Council and being given a place of trust, if not power, by the Hei of Fakhouri who had ruled as Royal Hei for nearly four hundred years. Now, the royal crown rests on the shoulders of Fakhouri blood born to the Naddar name. They are the ruling Hei and despite being currently near indistinguishable from their Fakhouri lineage, the Naddar have their own identity to seek and build... Standards of Practice
PoliticsAs the ruling hei of K'm't, Naddar has the greatest authority in politics. The pharaoh of K'm't is not merely a king but recognised as a god on earth. This makes them the ultimate power and decision-maker, regardless of the influence, advice or persuasion of those around them.
TradeBy law, the pharaoh technically owns all the land of K'm't. Any taxes or economic profit from those lands goes to the royal treasury which is, in turn, the Naddar's personal coin purse. They are, therefore, the richest family in the known land even if they are not personally involved in the trading business.
MilitaryThe current pharaoh is a military commander in his own right but the pharaoh is also the commander-in-chief of his forces by default. This means he has access to the entire kemetic military whenever he wishes.
ReligionAs a literal god on earth, the pharaoh is also the head of all temples and their assigned festivals, rituals and traditions. He is the earthly representative of the rest of the pantheon and is outwardly worshipped by his people as a deity. This power is shared by the Naddar house as a whole and its members are expected to be active participants in this grand public ceremonies.

A golden Hei from which money mounds and information flows, Sheifa holds many cards in the council and court of K'm't. Their merchant empire is one of the largest in the land and stretches far beyond kemetic borders to arabian lands and european kingdoms. Along these trading routes comes fine goods, shining coin and useful information. With a combination of the three, the women of Sheifa dictate the current of social engagements with their gossip and generosity, whilst the men hold powerful standing in the Pharaoh's Council of Ministers. The only chink in the Sheifa's powerful social armour is that of a recent scandal. A scandal that has revealed their matriarch to be the birth mother of the new heir apparent to the Pharaoh's crest. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThe house of Sheifa has, until recently, been hugely respected in most social circles of K'm't. Merchants with a vast trading network, Sheifa's influence stretches far and wide - including to the highest political powers and councilmen. Recent revelations have dented this reputation a little but, on the flipside, have now made the Sheifa matriarch the birth mother of the heir to the pharaoh's throne. This is a strange but highly powerful position to find oneself.
TradeTrade is the Sheifa's main focus. They are the only household to rival Isazari for their expertise in imports and exports, trading law and profit negotiation. Their connections stretch far into other lands and can be used as a network of information or spies.
MilitaryThough not actively involved in the military, Sheifa deal in a lot of weaponry military supplies. This brings them into military circles even if they have no authority there.
ReligionThe matriarch of the Sheifa hei has been instrumental over the years in carrying out the religious and public ceremonies of Thebes and the capital. Not one to draw focus, she has always been the support in the background of such matters, but her contributtion was always obvious to those paying attention. Whether this will continue after her recent scandal or not is unclear...
