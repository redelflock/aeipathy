INTRO Standards of Practice
PoliticsAshina is the ruling family of Babylonia so is deeply involved in the politics and ruling decisions of the land. They are the ultimate authority on Chaldean law and political matters.
TradeThough trade is not their forte, the Ashina family have a vast generational wealth ready to be invested and applied all over the land. This has brought them a strong, personal trading economy. On top of this, as the reigning household, Ashina also decide on the tax policies, trading laws and international exporting / importing regulations.
MilitaryThe monarch of Babylonia is officially and by default the leader of the centralised, national military. Even through the family itself does not have a strong military history or experience, they have full access to the entire state's military power.
ReligionAs rulers of the state, the Ashina family are also the official head of the religious system and temples. They have a strong connect to the priesthood and many of the women in the Ashina family have served in the temples.
Provincial Territories
No large territories / provinces currently assigned. Domestic Relations
BalthasarAshina and Balthasar have strong trading ties and are making one another richer by the minute. There is also a familial tie as Balthasar's matriarch is a close relative to the current monarchy. A small tension between the houses is that many believe +roshanak to be a better choice of queen.
KinsallaKinsalla is a deeply loyal Babylonian family with a long reputation of faithful military service. Their advice and position is respected in the Advisory Council.
Foreign Relations
AssyriaStrong political and trading connections but with a history of military conflict and territory claims.

HattusaWith Assyria between Hattusa and Babylonia there is a low diplomatic / political connection between Ashina and the Hattusa nation.
HellasBabylonia's ties to Eastern trade makes their exports sought by many lands, including Hellas. Trade connections are specialised but lucrative between Ashina and Hellas families.
K'm'tTrading routes across Phoenicia do connect Ashina with the K'm't nation but such a journey is only made for specialised trade such as for lapis lazuli.
