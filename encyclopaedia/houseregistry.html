Legend tells of a sippe so fierce and mighty that no man could lead them. In a contest of strength, to determine who might be capable of commanding such a powerful band of warriors, all contestants were overshadowed by a ferocious black bear, drawn down from the mountains by the gods. The people granted the bear reign over their territory and the kings of the Bearkings sippe are said to be descendants of the great beast. Now, the Bearkings sippe is known for its vast territories, large populace and insurmountable fighting force. With so many warriors ready to heed the call to battle, few dare to take on the children of the mighty bear. Standards of Practice
PoliticsPolitically, the Bearkings have in recent generations championed a tactical and thoughtful approach to legal matters and diplomacy. With no need to prove their might, they now look for peace with their fellow sippes and only turn their claws on enemies to the south.
TradeThe Bearkings have one of the largest reserves of wealth in Ostjord thanks to numerous arable lands within their territory. They have a large influence in the grain and food trade across Ostjord.
MilitaryIn battle, the Bearkings are ferocious and unwavering fighters but even more dangerous in their sheer numbers. Their shield wall is famous for being impenetrable thanks to the number of shoulders bearing it up.
ReligionThough the gods are very real and honoured appropriately, the Bearkings are one of the least spiritual sippes, preferring to build their own fortune and fate.

In the farthest and darkest valleys of the cragged Osthoj mountains lies the home of the Blacklock Hill sippe. Ruled under an iron boot and proficient in the deadliest of arts, it is well known that members of Blacklock Hill should never be trusted. Assassination, sadistic warfare techniques, poison, curses and heinous crimes against humanity… Blacklock Hill sees no value in mercy. Though they are rumoured to enjoy cruelty for cruelty’s sake, the truth is that warriors of Blacklock Hill are merely ambitious and unwilling to yield in their goals for the sake of human compassion. They are also, however, fiercely loyal to those they consider kin and are more than willing to make a colourful, bloody show of vengeance if necessary. Standards of Practice
PoliticsPolitically, Blacklock Hill are pretty cut throat. The only way to rule over a group so willing to kill for their ends is to be the most heartless of all. To that purpose, the laws of Blacklock Hill are specific and punishment for transgressing them harsh and rapid.
TradeThe mountains of Blacklock Hill are rich with coal and buildable stone. This is often traded for grain and flour from further south. This has provided Blacklock Hill with reasonable wealth but does not make them a heavy merchantile sippe.
MilitaryOn the battlefield, the warriors of Blacklock Hill are known for their lack of hesitation and mercy. They are swift, lethal and seem to openly enjoy killing.
ReligionReligiously, Blacklock Hill are quite devout. They practice their rituals faithfully and take fugaras and oracles very seriously.

The Bonemakers are an unyielding sort of people, tough and stubborn. Their territories are some of the toughest upon hillsides and mountains under dead woodland and over dark caverns. Yet they refuse to give in to the aggression of nature and maintain a way of life that breeds strong fighters and mighty women. They are known for their survival skills, ability to hunt large prey on mountain passes and their impressive knowledge of terrain and weather. A Bonemaker may not be the most vicious on the battlefield or the most connected to the gods, but barring a poisoning or lightning strike, they will outlast their rivals. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThe Bonemakers champion a very democratic system. The konungr is the final adjudicator but most trials and punishments are decided by open voting. Their laws are known to be firm but fair.
TradeThe Bonemakers are mostly self-sufficient and have little to do with the larger trading circles between the sippes.
MilitaryBonemakers are an aggressive bunch with a will to survive that seems almost super human at times. They are hard to defend against and even harder to put in the ground.
ReligionOf the different sippes, the Bonemakers have a fairly middle-of-the-road and balanced approach to worship; respecting the gods without making them the central axis of their community.

Twenty years ago the Firetongues sippe led a venture west that was promised to bear riches and bounty for all involved. It was a controversial move - a raid to the western isles of Briton instead of the tried and proven routes east and south. But the leader of the Firetongues was convinced of his choice. The voyage was a doomed obe, plagued by storms. And the landing was worse, facing the Saxon armies and fortress cities. Though the campaign lost the Firetongues almost all their royal family, a large measure of their fighting force and the respect of their fellow sippes, it did gain them a foothold in the western isles: the city of Beodricsworth. Ever since, the Firetongues have used this stronghold, their limited lands back home and their uncontested skills as master blacksmiths and crafters to forge a new future and new inheritance for the next generation. Standards of Practice
PoliticsFor the last two decades, the Firetongues have not had the luxury of harsh leadership. Careful diplomacy abd creative punishments have been utilised to ensure no further loss of men without unpinning the lead family’s authority. Now, a careful, diplomatic and occasionally democratic style of leadership is most common in this sippe.
TradeThe Firetongues are skilled craftsmen - particularly in metalwork. Their name comes from the way they seem able to communicate with the flames of the smithy forge to create spectacular and long-lasting accessories and weaponry. This is their main source of economic trade.
MilitaryIn combat, the Firetongues are armed with the finest weaponry and armour pieces. And they are hardly known as weaklings. But, equally, they do not share the fearsome reputation of other more military-focused sippes.
ReligionReligiously, the Firetongues are devout practitioners of ritual and pray to their Norse gods regularly and with care.
