A family of stoic reserve and glorious victory, Antonis is arguably the Athenian House most well-regarded. Alongside the Marikas name, Antonis claims one of the longest-standing bloodlines not just in Athenia but in all of Hellas. They have often sat upon the Athenian throne in some manner but their focus has never been political ambition but been focused, instead, on battlefields both near and far. For generations, the upper ranks of the Athenian military have been dominated by males of the Antonis line. They have a heavy presence in the capital's Guard, and they are often the primary authority on the protection detail of the monarchy. Their lack of personal greed makes their loyalty dependable and trustworthy. Standards of Practice
PoliticsThough the Antonis have no active desire to wear a crown, their repute is so renowned and their opinion so respected that they are often turned to in the Senate and Court. They are know to be firm but fair, surprisingly well-versed in the laws of the land and (even more shockingly) quick to champion a diplomatic solution before bloodshed becomes necessary.
TradeAntonis is not a house focused heavily on trade but their longevity has brought them copious numbers of provinces. The trade, exports, taxes etc of these provincial territories has made them very wealthy over the centuries. To keep their militia well stocked and fed, they must also trade for weaponry and horses with other lands. This places Antonis at the centre of a large economic network, even if they personally would not be considered merchants.
MilitaryThis is the absolute pinnacle of Antonis accomplishment. Their military units are the best trained, best equipped and most disciplined in Athenia. They are victorious in almost every conflict, hold the highest military ranks in the land and are well known for treating their fallen enemies with dignity. The men of the Antonis house are also famed for their skills aboard a chariot and often compete in the annual games in the capital.
ReligionFaith and its accompanying traditions and rituals are not massively significant in the houses of Antonis. Though they are, equally, never ignored. A deeply ingrained discipline and sense of propriety ensures that all appropriate temple practices are carried out exactly as they should be.

One of the oldest and most respected royal bloodlines of Athenia, Marikas have claimed the throne numerous times in the kingdom's history, building a reputation of disctinction, pride and elegance. Each generation is raised with the intention to one day marry abck into the royal line or claim the inheritance of the crown for themselves. With this in mind, the children of Marikas are always highly-educated, well-accomplished and funneled into the most powerful positions in Court and the Senate. Politics are discussed at the dining table and games of strategy woven into simple kids games. With the largest geographical area under their control, Marikas's intergenerational wealth is eye-watering and the military they can summon on command vast. Their resources, experience and intellect makes the Marikas house massively influential on matters of state and, by some, often treated like the 'real' rulers of the land. Standards of Practice
PoliticsPolitically, the Marikas house are untouchable and ironclad. Their resources are the greatest in the land, their experience in state matters the longest-standing. Their voice in the Senate is often spoken with quiet dignity but treated like a siren: loud and impossible to ignore. Equally, the Marikas women hold immense sway in the Athenian Court and play their own side of the coin with just as much authority.
TradeDespite their success in other areas of courtier life, the Marikas house probably would consider themselves traders. The size of their lands, the businesses, taxes and trading within them and their network of connections, both domestic and foreign, ensure that money flows through Marikas like a golden river. It takes great skill, intellect and organisation to ensure a coin does not go astray.
MilitaryBecause of the size of Marikas's land, they could technically raise the largest military force in Athenian. However, they do not (in the last few generations at least) have the same military experience and capability as a house like Antonis.
ReligionAfter centuries of leading the capital's religious festivals and temple traditions, the Marikas continue to be deeply entwined with the practical duties of the Hellenic faith, even if they are not the ruling house anymore.

Despite holding the least personal reserves and provincial resources, the Xanthos house has found themselves at the peak of Athenia's political mountain. As monarchs of the Athenian kingdom, they lead the Senate, manage the Court and oversee the care of its people through all civil and legal matters. This is a duty that the Xanthos house takes very seriously. For the last few generations, children of the household have been trained in all areas of the Senate and civic service. They are taught that a position of power is actually one of servitude: to aid and support those who rely upon them. Despite longer-standing bloodlines eyeing the crown for themselves, the impeccable record of Xanthos's rule has prevented any outright coups. Now, however, they face a future of princesses - with no male heir to continue the ruling line. Should tradition be respected and the crown fall to a young courtier openly regarded as irresponsible and selfish? Or can the Xanthos tactically arrange a small and nuanced shift in law that would delegate the management of Athenia and its people to Princess Persephone: a young woman already recognised as a gentle and competent successor? Standards of Practice
PoliticsSeveral generations ago, the Xanthos house claimed the throne through means that were not illegal but certainly suspicious. The following generations, however, seem to have inherited none of that personal ambition. Instead, they see rulership as a service; a means of helping others and securing the future of those in their care. They are known to be fair, diplomatic and thoughtful - never weak but certainly tempered with mercy, compassion and creative solutions.
TradeThe Xanthos has little personal wealth and even less household trading networks. Instead, they manage the taxes, treasury, exports and imports of the kingdom as a whole. This gives them access to a massive volume of resources but only whilst they retain the monarchy.
MilitaryAgain, the Xanthos have little by way of military power or expertise in their own right. As monarch of the land, King Minas has the ultimate power over the entire Athenian military but only whilst he still wears the crown.
ReligionAs head of the political and social pyramids of the kingdom, the Xanthos family must lead the nation in religious matters of state, such as public festivals, rituals or demonstrations. They are very devouted to this practice and have strong personal penitence for many of the Hellenic gods, such as Hera, Athena, Hestia and Poseidon.

The House of Nikolaos is often considered to be strong and dependable. They don't often make waves, they don't often cause political stirs but they are widely recognised as a strong and powerful pillar upon which many other Houses and provinces rely. Led with the belief in careful thought, efficient dedication and the importance of inner strength and integrity, the House and its members are considered to be both intelligent administrators and skilled mediators or negotiators. Always calm and rarely seen out of sorts, the current patriarch is a clear and obvious example of this mentality. Originally a family of legal administrators at the Magnestis docks, the Nikolaos are skilled people-pleasers and efficient politicians whose sole aim is to help build a strong and supportive base for the Athenian political circle. Standards of Practice
PoliticsPolitics is really Nikolaos's greatest asset. Not because they are greedy or manipulate matters for their own ends but because they believe in the open discussion, democratic observances and passionate debate that is the foundation of the Hellenic polis method of rule. They are skilled talkers but equally talented listeners. They are known for mediating debates, calming arguments and suggesting compromises that had previously been invisible to both parties. They hold more sway and respect in the Senate than normal for a noble house, strictly because of their selfless reputation - that their words are fuelled by the desire to benefit all, not only themselves.
TradeAs leaders of a port province, the Nikolaos are knee deep in trade and the secondary branch of the family manage these matters with care. They are skilled accountants and well-educated in export tax and trade legalities. This, however, is all on a small, provincial level.
MilitaryNikolaos have no deep investment or ties to the military, even though a few of their sons have chosen it as their profession. The nephew of the Nikolaos patriarch is currently the leader of the Athenian Guard.
ReligionThe women of the Nikolaos house are active in Court and do their part for religious festivities and traditions. This has not gone unnoticed by the royal ladies of society and has only added to their good reputation.
