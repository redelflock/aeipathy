OrthrusA lesser-known but still fearsome two-headed dog, often guarding remote shrines or monstrous lairs. Known to be fiercely loyal and unnaturally fast, it's said to be nearly impossible to kill unless both heads are struck at once. Similar to a hell hound. PegasusA winged stallion of divine origin, often glimpsed only by those marked for destiny. Said to be born from blood and sky, its hooves spark springs and its flight is a symbol of freedom and fate. Many debate on whether pegasus is a singular creature or a race of majestic equines kept by the gods. ScyllaA grotesque sea creature haunting narrow straits, she has six serpent-like necks, each ending in a snapping maw. She is said to haunt charybdis's wake so attempting to avid a scylla will likely send you into the charybdis's vortex. SirensCruel and tricksome creatures known to take on the appearance and voice of irresistible women. They lure sailors into trances with their song, intent on crashing their ships against shallows and rocks, then stealing their cargo. SphinxA majestic but menacing guardian of secrets and riddles, with the body of a lion and the face of a woman. She lies in wait near ancient ruins and those who cannot answer her questions are devoured. Her riddles are said to be woven with prophecy and to have lingering meaning for you personally even after solving them. TyphonA colossal titan-like monster with a thousand eyes, Typhon is supposedly all-seeing and all-knowing but currently trapped beneath a mountain by Zeus. His fiery breath and writhing limbs still shake the earth in times of great turmoil, either in anger or warning. LamiaOnce a beautiful forgotten queen of a forgotten people, now a cursed predator who preys on minors. With serpentine features and a hunger for blood, she wanders desolate places at night, whispered about by mothers to frighten naughty children. MinotaurThe bull-headed man-like creature locked deep within labyrinths or labyrinthine cave systems. Ancient rumors say it feeds on sacrifices sent in as tribute, and that the maze of tunnels it lives in will shift to protect its monstrous inhabitant. GorgonsFeared and avoided even in myth, these cursed women bear serpents for hair and petrifying stares. Said to dwell in remote caves or forgotten temples, their mere gaze turns men and women to stone — yet some foolish individuals seek them deliberately, hoping to harness their deadly power. GriffinNoble and terrifying creatures with the body of a lion and the head and wings of an eagle. Said to guard treasures and ancient secrets in mountain caves or sky-temples. They’re both revered and feared by adventurers. HarpiesVile, bird-like women who descend from the skies to steal food, souls or secrets. Known for their screeching wails and foul stench, they often serve as omens of doom or divine punishment. HydraA serpentine beast lurking in fetid swamps, infamous for its regenerative heads — cut one off and two more grow in its place. Villagers avoid its lair, fearful of the venomous breath said to rot flesh from bone and its poisonous blood said to kill at the slightest touch. KrakensMonstrous sea leviathans whispered about in coastal taverns, said to rise from the deep and drag whole ships into the abyss with their massive, coiling tentacles. Some sailors swear they’ve seen the eye of one, the size of a man’s shield, beneath the waves.
