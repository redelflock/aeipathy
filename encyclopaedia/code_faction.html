[u]Faction Name[/u]

[u]Faction Sigil[/u] What sigil or crest would this faction have upon its shield? Please note that this is subject to availability - check our Factions and Factions page (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/596/) to avoid suggesting duplicates.

[u]Faction Colours[/u] What colours would the faction shield have? Please note that this is subject to availability - check our Factions and Factions page (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/596/) to avoid suggesting duplicates.

[u]Faction Motto[/u] This needs to be a few words / the motto of the faction. For example, the Athenian Guard's motto is 'By Athena's Grace'. Try to keep this phrase as just a few words (and no more than five-ish at max).

[u]Faction Blurb[/u] This needs to be roughly 250-400 words regarding the faction, its recent history or origin (if either are particularly note-worthy) and its general feel, ethos, trade, purpose and intent. Think of this blurb as a summary of everything else in this section. If you are uncertain what to write in this, please check our Factions and Factions page (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/596) to see other examples.

[u]Land of Origin / Operation[/u] Where was the faction founded and do they still operate in that area or have they moved? Are they are travelling cult or an espionage effort beyond the borders of their homeland?

[u]Faction Ethos[/u] Please give a few morals or values that the House believes in. These should be short and sweet sentences that sum up their House's main beliefs. E.g., 'Vows are unbreakable.' 'Money is the greatest power.' etc...

[u]Reputation[/u] Please choose four singular words (not sentences) that others might use to describe the members of this House. These might not be descriptors the House themselves would use: it is about how others see them. Are they 'arrogant', 'well-connected', 'wealthy', 'stoic' etc.?

[u]Politics[/u] If your faction is politically-inclined, write a short paragraph of their mission, efforts and practices in the halls of power and the kinds of plots this might inspire.

OR -- [u]Military[/u] If your faction is militarily-inclined, write a short paragraph of their mission, efforts and practices on the battlefield and the kinds of plots this might inspire.

OR -- [u]Economics[/u] If your faction is trade-inclined, write a short paragraph of their mission, efforts and practices in economic trade and business and the kinds of plots this might inspire.

[u]Preferred Plots[/u] Please write a little in here about what kind of global plots you would like this House to be a part of. What prompted the creation of this House? How can they fit into the global plot we currently have on-going? This information will help the staff team to create Events and threads in which the members of this House can participate, so you can explore the plots you want to.

[u]Faction History[/u] Please write roughly 600 words on the history of the faction. How did it come to be? Why was it established? Who backed its founding or was against it? How has it come to the 'level' at which it now exists? What is the structure / organisation of the faction?

[u]Primary Roles[/u] Please list any significant ranks / roles within the faction. This might include leaders of the group, those who rule over records or faith practices, a particular name given to new recruits etc. For ideas, check out our Factions and Factions page () for other entries.

[u]Joining the Faction[/u] Most organisations have some form of initiation process / ritual. Describe how someone might come to be a member of the faction or (if it's a secret one) how they might even come to know of the faction's existence.

[u]Practice / Ritual #1[/u] For each contribution to our lore, we ask for detail and description. To build realistic texture into the faction, please write a short paragraph description of three particular rituals the faction carries out. These might be part of a new member's training, an annual celebration, something related to faith, something connected to the morals and ethics of the organisation.

[u]Practice / Ritual #2[/u] See above.

[u]Wanted Characters[/u] We ask that all of our contribution submissions come with plotting opportunities for other members. Please copy and paste the below code to include 2+ living characters already attached to the faction. This short list might include the leader of the group, or a character tied to a particularly dramatic plot within the faction. There's no maximum of how many wanted ads you can build attached to the faction but it must be a minimum of two.

[u]Face Claim[/u] Please check our Face Claim list (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/529) to ensure that this face is not already chosen for another played, canon or wanted character. If you have any confusion over this, please message a staff member regarding your choices.
[u]Character Blurb[/u] This blurb must read as a wanted ad, for that is what it will become upon the acceptance of the faction. It must be over 300 words in length and cover the character's basic history, familial connections, personality, hobbies etc. and any plot potential they hold. Remember: use the information you have written above for the faction to aid in the creation of the character. Remember to ensure that these characters are written in a manner that befits the environment that they grew up in and have been exposed to.

[u]Canon Connections[/u] In order to ensure that our contributions do not get cut off from our canon world, we require all of our factions to have two connections to canons in some way, shape or form. For example... one of your faction's characters might have a rivalry with a royal son who they trained with in the militia, another might be a secret admirer of a royal lady or had an affair with said lady to the shame and scandal of all. The connection can be with a particular character or a House as a whole. But there must be two of them and they cannot be with the same house. We recommend that you put a shout out on our Discord server (https://discord.gg/2f2MZP3G) or seek out members who actively play canon characters (check our Compendium - https://aeipathyrp.com/board/477) in order to make this 'connection' with active players or at least with open canon characters that have a close connection to active characters. This is all about building a springboard plot for your faction's launch. Make sure to include:
- What the connection is.
- Who the connection is between.
- Which members you have spoken with to finalise this idea.
- How you see it playing out in the future (this doesn't have to be set in stone - give us a range of fun ideas you intend to throw at the wall and we look forward to seeing what sticks).

[u]Contribution Disclaimer[/u] Please confirm with a simple 'Yes' that you understand our rules for world contributions in our Fundamental Rules guide (https://aeipathyrp.com/board/583) and that this faction, its information and its characters, upon acceptance, will become part of Aeipathy's lore and owned by the site.[/div]
