Despite the union being arranged initially, it is commonly thought in the Athenian courts that the following marriage of the King and Queen was one of love and respect. King Minas has cancelled the Senate appearances for the next week and retired to his rooms to mourn. Queen Lucille also leaves behind her two daughters, Princess Persephone and Princess Emilia.
